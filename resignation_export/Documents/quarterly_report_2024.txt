=== CyberLeap Q3 2024 Security Posture Report ===
Classification: INTERNAL USE ONLY

Executive Summary:
Q3 2024 showed a 15% reduction in security incidents compared to Q2.
The vulnerability management program processed 342 findings across
87 assets. Mean time to remediate (MTTR) decreased from 12.3 days
to 9.7 days.

Key Metrics:
- Total incidents: 23 (down from 27)
- Critical vulnerabilities patched: 12
- Penetration tests conducted: 4
- Compliance assessments: 2 (SOC 2, ISO 27001)
- Security awareness training completion: 94%

Ongoing Concerns:
- Legacy systems in DMZ require architecture review
- Third-party vendor risk assessments are behind schedule
- Cloud IAM policies need consolidation

Prepared by: Security Operations Team
Date: October 15, 2024
