Section 7: Information Security

7.1 Acceptable Use
All employees must use company resources solely for authorized
business purposes. Personal use of company systems is permitted
to a reasonable extent but must not compromise security.

7.2 Data Classification
- PUBLIC: Marketing materials, public product info
- INTERNAL: Business documents, internal communications
- CONFIDENTIAL: Client data, financial records, credentials
- RESTRICTED: Cryptographic keys, master credentials

7.3 Incident Reporting
All suspected security incidents must be reported to the SOC
within 4 hours of discovery via the incident portal or by
emailing soc@cyberleap.io.

7.4 Clean Desk Policy
Employees must secure sensitive documents when leaving their
workstation. Screens must be locked after 5 minutes of inactivity.

7.5 Departure Procedures
Upon resignation or termination, all company data must be
returned and access credentials revoked within 24 hours.
